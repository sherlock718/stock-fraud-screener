What do the colored tags mean?

Value — Is the stock cheap or expensive?
Quality — Is the company well-run and profitable?
Fraud — Are there signs of manipulation or distress?
Momentum — Is the stock price trending up or down?
Growth — Is the company growing or shrinking?
Economy — What's happening in the broader economy?
Combos — Two signals combined (e.g., cheap + profitable)
Target — What we're trying to predict (future returns)
